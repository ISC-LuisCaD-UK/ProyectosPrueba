Home made hotdogs

We are going to prepare delicious home-made hot dogs. They are super easy to do and all the ingredients can be bought in every mexican supermarket.

Ingredients

  • Bimbo hot dog bread (midnights)
  • Rosarito beef hot dog sausages
  • Sliced bacon
  • Chopped tomato
  • Chopped letuce
  • Mayonaise and mustard

Instructions

  1. Roll a slice of bacon on every sausage, depending on the number of hot dogs you want
  2. Fry the bacon rolled sausages
  3. Heat the hot dog bread on a flat pan or preferently on a vapor caserole
  4. Turn the sausages from time to time until well fried, avoiding turning then into charcoal
  5. Apply mayo and mustard inside the bread
  6. Put a fried sausage in every bread
  7. Put some chopped letuce and tomato over de sausages and apply a little mayo on top
  8. You can add other seasoning ingredients of your choice
  9. Enjoy!
